Our Story

Forbes Cosmetics was founded in 2015 with a simple goal: make high-quality makeup that works for real people with real skin. What began as a single lipstick line has grown into a full range of face, eye, and lip products loved by customers around the world.

Our Mission

We believe makeup should be inclusive, affordable, and fun. Our mission is to keep expanding our shade ranges and formulas so that everyone can find their perfect match.
